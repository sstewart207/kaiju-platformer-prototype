# Ruins playtest

About 5 minutes · Build `1080p default` · October 27, 2026

[Open the game in Chrome or Edge](https://sstewart207.github.io/kaiju-platformer-prototype/). No game account or installation needed.

[Shared checklist and results](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13i3q_Qcw7wFqONVrRR1tr06k5bPceDKe_HdHKdy9HJU/edit?tab=t.6elj8c648aa8). Add results there; leave earlier entries in place. Shane and Codex review new entries, record fixes and request retests.

## Controls

- Keyboard: WASD/arrows move · Space jump · Shift dash · Esc pause · R restart.
- DualSense: stick/D-pad move · Cross jump/select · Square or R1 dash · Options pause · Triangle restart.

## Checks

1. **Movement:** Stand still for 10 seconds, then move in each direction and release. Check for drift, sliding or awkward steering.
2. **Jump and dash:** Compare a tapped jump with a held jump. Try repeated dashes, then jump and dash together. Note unwanted movement or collisions.
3. **Course:** Reach the finish. Try the walking route and moving-platform shortcut if you can. Note confusing turns, hard-to-read landings or getting stuck.
4. **Recovery:** Step off a raised ledge. Check that you land safely or recover. Pause/resume, switch tabs and return, then restart. Check for stuck controls.
5. **Overall:** Note stutters, camera problems, character visibility and whether you would replay.
